# ATS Best Practices

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) parse resumes before a human ever sees them. Resumes that
ignore ATS conventions are often rejected automatically, regardless of the candidate's
actual qualifications.

Use standard section headings such as "Experience", "Education", "Skills", and "Projects".
Unusual or creative headings (e.g. "My Journey", "What I Bring") are often not recognized.

Avoid tables, text boxes, columns, and images for critical content. Many ATS parsers read
PDFs linearly and can scramble or drop content placed in multi-column layouts or graphics.

Use standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman) and avoid special characters, icons, or
symbols in place of bullet points where possible.

Save and submit resumes as PDF or DOCX unless the job posting specifies otherwise. Avoid
scanned images of resumes, since ATS cannot extract text from them.

Include exact keywords from the job description where truthful and relevant, including both
the spelled-out term and common acronyms (e.g. "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)").

Avoid headers and footers for contact information, since some ATS systems do not parse them.

Keep formatting consistent: one font family, consistent bullet style, consistent date format
(e.g. "Jan 2023 - Present").
